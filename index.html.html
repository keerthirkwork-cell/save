Who We Are

About Design AID Engineering

Design AID Engineering is a engineering services company specializing in Engineering R&D Solutions. With a strong foundation in design and technical expertise, we provide end-to-end product development support through a global delivery model.

Our team's decades of experience span Consumer & Healthcare, Aerospace & Defence, Industrial, Transportation, and Electromechanical Products — combining innovation with efficiency to reduce costs and accelerate time-to-market while improving product quality.
